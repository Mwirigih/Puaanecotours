9 days

Tailored Safari: Migration Time

Nairobi, Ol Pejeta, Lake Elementaita, Maasai Mara

Itinerary

  1. Day 1Arrive at JKIA, Nairobi.
  2. Day 2Full-day Nairobi excursion: Nairobi National Park, the David Sheldrick Wildlife Trust, Giraffe Centre, museum visits, and lunch at a selected restaurant.
  3. Day 3Nairobi to Ol Pejeta Conservancy for a two-night stay at East and Central Africa's largest rhino sanctuary.
  4. Day 4Full-day game drives and wildlife experiences in Ol Pejeta Conservancy.
  5. Day 5Ol Pejeta to Lake Elementaita. Spend the afternoon with flamingos, diverse birdlife, and the rare Rothschild's giraffe.
  6. Day 6Lake Elementaita to Maasai Mara National Reserve. Arrive for a three-night stay.
  7. Day 7Full-day Maasai Mara safari with a focus on the Great Migration.
  8. Day 8Another full day in the Maasai Mara, following the Great Migration.
  9. Day 9Return to Nairobi and transfer to JKIA for your outbound flight.
