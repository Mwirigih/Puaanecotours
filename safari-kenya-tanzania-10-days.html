10 days

East African Moment: Kenya and Tanzania

Amboseli, Ngorongoro, Serengeti, Maasai Mara

Itinerary

  1. Day 1Arrive at JKIA, Nairobi.
  2. Day 2Nairobi to Amboseli National Park. Arrive for a two-night stay and afternoon game drive.
  3. Day 3Full-day game drives in Amboseli National Park.
  4. Day 4Cross into Tanzania via the Namanga border and continue to the Ngorongoro Conservation Area.
  5. Day 5Explore the Ngorongoro Crater, Olduvai Gorge, and the Shifting Sands.
  6. Day 6Transfer to Serengeti National Park for game viewing.
  7. Day 7Full-day safari in the Serengeti, including the Grumeti region.
  8. Day 8Cross back into Kenya via the Isebania border and continue to the Maasai Mara National Reserve.
  9. Day 9Full-day safari in the Maasai Mara.
  10. Day 10Return to Nairobi for lunch and transfer to JKIA for your outbound flight.
