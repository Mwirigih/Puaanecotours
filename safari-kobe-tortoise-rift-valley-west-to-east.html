8 days

Kobe Tortoise Rift Valley Challenge: West to East

Lake Nakuru, Naivasha, Amboseli, Tsavo, Kenyan Coast

Itinerary

  1. Day 1Arrive in Nairobi on your late-night or early-morning flight.
  2. Day 2Nairobi to Lake Nakuru National Park. Arrive in time for lunch, then an afternoon game drive.
  3. Day 3Morning game drive, then on to Lake Naivasha. Enjoy a boat ride, a guided walk on Crescent Island, and an optional visit to Hell's Gate National Park.
  4. Day 4Lake Naivasha to Amboseli National Park. Arrive in time for lunch.
  5. Day 5Full-day game drives in Amboseli National Park.
  6. Day 6Amboseli to Tsavo West National Park. Visit Mzima Springs and the Shetani Lava Flow.
  7. Day 7Tsavo West to Tsavo East National Park, famous for its red elephants. Optional visit to the Voi Elephant Orphanage.
  8. Day 8Depart for the Kenyan Coast to relax and enjoy the beach.
