8 days

Kenya Classic Safari

Nairobi, Samburu, Aberdare, Lake Nakuru, Maasai Mara

Itinerary

  1. Day 1Arrive at Jomo Kenyatta International Airport (JKIA), Nairobi.
  2. Day 2Nairobi to Samburu National Reserve. Lunch on arrival and afternoon game drive.
  3. Day 3Full-day game drives in Samburu National Reserve.
  4. Day 4Samburu to Aberdare National Park. Lunch at Aberdare Country Club, overnight at The Ark Lodge.
  5. Day 5Aberdare to Lake Nakuru National Park. Afternoon game drive and overnight.
  6. Day 6Lake Nakuru to Maasai Mara National Reserve. En-route game drive, arrive for lunch.
  7. Day 7Full-day safari in the Maasai Mara.
  8. Day 8Return to Nairobi. Optional city activities before airport transfer for departure.
