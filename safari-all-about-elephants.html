10 days

All About Elephants

Samburu, Sheldrick elephant camps, Tsavo East, Amboseli

Itinerary

  1. Day 1Arrive at JKIA, Nairobi.
  2. Day 2Nairobi to Samburu National Reserve. Arrive for a game drive and overnight stay.
  3. Day 3Visit the Reteti Elephant Sanctuary with a picnic lunch. Optional visit to a Samburu cultural village.
  4. Day 4Full-day game drives in Samburu National Reserve.
  5. Day 5Return to Nairobi. Visit Nairobi National Park, the David Sheldrick Wildlife Trust, and shop for supplies for the Umani/Ithumba elephant camps.
  6. Day 6Spend the day at the Sheldrick elephant camps for the morning, midday, and evening feeding sessions.
  7. Day 7Depart early for Tsavo East National Park.
  8. Day 8Full-day game drives in Tsavo East, home to Kenya's famous red elephants.
  9. Day 9Tsavo East to Amboseli National Park. En-route game drive, arriving for lunch.
  10. Day 10Return to Nairobi from Amboseli for your departure flight, or extend with an optional extra night in Amboseli.
